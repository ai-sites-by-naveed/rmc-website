EHS Audits & Safety / Fire Gap Assessments

Comprehensive baseline walkthroughs and code-referenced gap assessments for factories, pharmaceutical units, warehouses, and multi-storey commercial towers. We quantify physical hazards, egress bottlenecks, electrical ignition exposures, and statutory non-conformities before regulators or international buyer auditors flag them.

Core Deliverables
Photo-evidenced EHS & Fire Gap Register, Critical/High/Medium Risk Matrix, marked floor egress & fire protection deficiency drawings, and time-bound CAPA tracker.
Codes & Standards
Sindh Occupational Safety & Health Act 2017 & Rules 2019, Building Code of Pakistan (Fire Safety Provisions 2016), NFPA 101, amfori BSCI, WRAP, and client buyer protocols.
Testing & Verification Protocol
Workplace illumination (lux), occupational noise dB(A), thermal hotspot checks on distribution boards, hydrant static/residual pressure check, and emergency exit travel verification.
Post-Delivery & Advisory Support
Executive management briefing, CAPA closure re-verification inspection, and regulatory/Civil Defence NOC documentation alignment.

EHS Management Systems & Certified Workforce Training

Practical EHS system engineering that lives on the shop floor rather than sitting in binders. We build structured ISO 45001 and ISO 14001 governance, HIRA/JSA registers, Permit-to-Work (PTW) controls, and deliver hands-on firefighting, evacuation, and chemical-handling drills tailored to each shift.

Core Deliverables
EHS Manual, Hazard Identification & Risk Assessment (HIRA), Environmental Aspect/Impact Register, Permit-to-Work & Lockout/Tagout SOPs, Emergency Response Plan, and training attendance/assessment logs.
Codes & Standards
ISO 45001:2018 (OHSMS), ISO 14001:2015 (EMS), Sindh OSH Rules 2019, and NFPA 600 / 10 industrial brigade training guidelines.
Training & Drill Protocol
Batch-wise practical sessions covering live fire extinguisher discharge (A/B/C/CO₂), hydrant hose handling, chemical spill containment, PPE discipline, first aid, and timed plant evacuation drills.
Post-Delivery & O&M Support
Internal auditor coaching, pre-certification Stage 1/Stage 2 mock audits, and annual refresher training retainers.

Wastewater Treatment Plants (ETP / STP) & Water Recycling

Turnkey process engineering, civil/electromechanical construction, commissioning, and ongoing Operation & Maintenance (O&M) of industrial Effluent Treatment Plants, Sewage Treatment Plants, and tertiary Ultrafiltration / Reverse Osmosis (UF/RO) water recovery systems built for Pakistan's textile, dyeing, pharma, and process corridors.

Core Deliverables
Effluent characterization & treatability study, Process Flow Diagrams (PFD), P&IDs, civil tankage & baffle works, MBBR/ASP aeration grids, chemical dosing skids, filter press dewatering, and tertiary UF/RO recycling units.
Codes & Standards
Sindh Environmental Protection Agency (SEPA) / SEQS, National Environmental Quality Standards (NEQS), ZDHC Wastewater Guidelines (Foundational–Aspirational), and DRAP cGMP utility controls.
Testing & Commissioning Protocol
Laboratory jar testing, hydraulic leak & retention time verification, MLSS biological stabilization, and SEPA-approved third-party composite lab verification (pH, BOD₅, COD, TSS, TDS, O&G, heavy metals).
O&M & Lifecycle Support
Dedicated shift plant operators, daily dosing optimization, sludge handling management, RO membrane CIP cleaning, and blower/pump preventive maintenance contracts.

Fire Hydrant & Automatic Fire Suppression Systems

End-to-end hydraulic design, fabrication, trenching, installation, hydrotesting, and O&M of pressurized external/internal fire hydrant ring mains, NFPA 20 pump rooms, sprinkler/deluge networks, and clean-agent / CO₂ / foam suppression systems for critical industrial assets.

Core Deliverables
Hydraulic flow/head calculations, pump room fabrication (Electric + Diesel + Jockey), welded & epoxy-coated MS/HDPE hydrant ring mains, landing valves, hose reels, sprinkler risers, and clean-agent (FM-200 / Novec) or CO₂/foam skids.
Codes & Standards
NFPA 13 (Sprinklers), NFPA 14 (Standpipes), NFPA 20 (Fire Pumps), NFPA 24 (Private Fire Mains), NFPA 2001 (Clean Agent), NFPA 11/12, and Building Code of Pakistan 2016.
Testing & Commissioning Protocol
12-bar / 1.5x working pressure hydrostatic pipeline test, main/diesel pump churn-100%-150% performance curve test, jockey cut-in/cut-out sequencing, and end-line pitot flow verification.
O&M & Readiness Support
Weekly diesel cranking & battery checks, valve exercising, pressure-gauge calibration, and annual flow-test certifications.

Fire Detection & Addressable Alarm Systems

Intelligent early-warning fire detection engineered to isolate the exact room, rack, or electrical panel in seconds — eliminating nuisance alarms while triggering coordinated HVAC shutdowns, fire pump interlocks, and multi-floor occupant evacuation.

Core Deliverables
Zone & detector coverage layouts, Conventional or Addressable Fire Alarm Control Panels (FACP), optical smoke & rate-of-rise heat detectors, Manual Call Points (MCPs), high-decibel bells/sounders, and fire-rated cabling.
Codes & Standards
NFPA 72 (National Fire Alarm and Signaling Code), Building Code of Pakistan 2016, and UL/EN-54 component alignment.
Testing & Commissioning Protocol
100% point-by-point aerosol smoke & controlled heat functional testing, open/short loop isolator checks, sound-pressure dB(A) audibility sweep, and 24-hour standby + alarm battery autonomy verification.
O&M & Calibration Support
Quarterly optical chamber dust cleaning (critical for textile lint environments), event-log audits, and rapid sensor/module replacement.

Fire Incident Investigation & Origin / Cause Forensics

"Find the Cause. Prevent the Next." — Rapid post-fire scene preservation, scientific burn-pattern analysis, and electrical arc mapping that move beyond superficial "short circuit" assumptions. We deliver objective forensic reports and Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA) redesigns for plant boards, insurers, and legal teams.

Core Deliverables
Chain-of-custody evidence log, Origin & Cause Forensic Investigation Report, electrical arc-mapping diagram, timeline reconstruction, and prioritized Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA) engineering redesign.
Codes & Methodology
NFPA 921 (Guide for Fire and Explosion Investigations) scientific method, NFPA 1033 investigator competency principles, and Building Code of Pakistan post-incident evaluation.
Field Investigation Protocol
Rapid 48-hour mobilization across Karachi/Sindh corridors, debris layering & excavation, panel breaker/conductor inspection, CCTV/BMS log correlation, and structured operator interviews.
Post-Delivery & Prevention Support
Board and insurance adjuster technical briefings, plus turnkey implementation of detection, suppression, and EHS controls to prevent recurrence.
