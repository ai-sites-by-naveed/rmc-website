Engineering & Compliance FAQ

Practical Answers Before Commissioning or Inspection

Clear operational guidance on how RMC structures audits, turnkey construction, ongoing O&M, and urgent post-incident deployments.

What happens during an RMC EHS & Fire Safety Gap Assessment?

RMC engineers conduct a physical walkthrough covering egress widths, fire loads, hydrant pressure and FACP zoning, chemical storage, electrical panels, and wastewater discharge points. You receive a photographic non-conformity register mapped to the Sindh OSH Act 2017, Building Code of Pakistan 2016, NFPA codes, and export buyer standards with prioritized corrective actions (CAPA).

Does RMC handle both ETP/Water Recycling construction and ongoing O&M?

Yes. RMC manages the entire wastewater lifecycle—process engineering, civil basins, mechanical and dosing installation, commissioning against SEPA SEQS parameters, tertiary UF/RO water recycling integration, and continuous daily plant operation & maintenance (O&M) with operator training.

Are your fire hydrant, detection, and suppression designs aligned with Building Code of Pakistan 2016 & NFPA?

All hydraulic calculations, pump room layouts (electric, diesel, jockey), ring mains, addressable fire alarm control panels (FACP), and clean-agent/CO₂/foam systems are engineered strictly to Building Code of Pakistan (Fire Safety Provisions 2016) alongside NFPA 13, 14, 20, 24, 72, and 2001.

How quickly can RMC mobilize for a post-fire incident investigation in Karachi or Sindh?

From our Saddar, Karachi headquarters, our investigation specialist can mobilize within 24–48 hours across Karachi industrial zones, Nooriabad, Hub, and Hyderabad, and rapidly nationwide. Call 0316-9043273 immediately so scene preservation and NFPA 921 arc mapping begin before debris clearance destroys physical origin evidence.

Can RMC help prepare our facility for SEPA compliance, Civil Defence NOC, or export buyer audits (ZDHC / BSCI)?

We close both engineering hardware gaps (ETP upgrades, operable hydrants, addressable detectors, signage) and documentation gaps (ISO 45001/14001 manuals, HIRA logs, evacuation drill records, SEPA-approved lab reports) required for SEPA monitoring, SBCA/Civil Defence NOCs, and buyer audits including ZDHC, amfori BSCI, and WRAP.
