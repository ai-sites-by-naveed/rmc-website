RMC-CS-01 Representative Benchmark

Textile Dye-House Effluent Treatment & 62% Water Recycling Plant

S.I.T.E. Industrial Area, Karachi · Textiles & Apparel

High-color, high-COD dyeing effluent (COD > 1,400 mg/L) risked SEPA enforcement while municipal water scarcity constrained shift capacity. Engineered an Equalization + Physicochemical + MBBR + Tertiary UF/RO skid with ongoing O&M.
