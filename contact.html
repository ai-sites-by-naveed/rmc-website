Urgent Post-Incident Protocol · NFPA 921

Emergency Post-Fire Investigation Notice

"Find the Cause. Prevent the Next." — Immediately after extinguishing a plant or warehouse fire, isolate electrical power safely, restrict non-essential cleanup, and preserve distribution panels, cabling, and debris burn patterns before disturbance.

Premature debris clearing destroys primary vs. secondary arc-mapping evidence. Call 0316-9043273 or 0316-9043272 immediately for rapid scene preservation and forensic mobilization.

Pre-Consultation Preparation Guide

Having these operational records ready allows RMC's principal engineers to deliver sharper feasibility estimates and commissioning schedules during the initial survey.

Fire Hydrant, Suppression & Addressable Alarm Projects (NFPA 13 / 20 / 72)

Prepare architectural floor layouts or CAD site plans showing total covered area, building height, occupancy hazard classification (textile storage, solvent room, high-rise office), existing municipal or bore-water reservoir capacity, and any prior SBCA or Civil Defence inspection notes.

Wastewater Treatment (ETP / STP) & Water Recycling Plants (SEPA / SEQS)

Gather recent grab or 24-hour composite raw effluent lab reports (pH, BOD₅, COD, TSS, TDS, color, oil & grease), peak daily discharge volume (m³/day), production process chemicals or dye classes, and the available ground footprint for civil tankage and UF/RO skids.

EHS Audits, Fire Gap Assessments & ISO 45001 / 14001 Systems

Keep existing safety policies, chemical inventory / SDS binders, past buyer CAPA lists (amfori BSCI, WRAP, ZDHC), headcount per shift, and prior fire drill or equipment maintenance logs ready for our gap baseline review.

Post-Fire Incident Investigation & Scene Preservation (NFPA 921)

Secure perimeter access, preserve untouched electrical switchboards and tripped breakers, retain CCTV or FACP event logs from before the incident, and prepare a witness roster (security, shift supervisors, maintenance electricians) for structured forensic interviews.
