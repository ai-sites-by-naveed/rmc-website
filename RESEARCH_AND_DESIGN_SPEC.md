## 2. Design Direction & Rationale

### 2.1 Visual Paradigm: Authoritative Swiss Engineering Grid + High-Trust Industrial Institutional

Generic SaaS gradients or soft rounded illustrations undermine engineering credibility in life-safety and industrial infrastructure. RMC's audience consists of factory directors, plant engineers, compliance heads, insurers, and facility owners evaluating technical competence for high-consequence fire and environmental systems.

The design direction pairs:
- **Swiss Architectural Grid Discipline**: Hairline `1px` structural grid borders, numbered index markers (`01`–`06`), crisp geometric spacing, and modular subgrid rows reminiscent of NFPA engineering schematics and ISO compliance dossiers.
- **High-Trust Industrial Institutional Tone**: Grounded in RMC's own LinkedIn palette—deep **Sovereign Industrial Navy** anchor bands contrasted with crisp **Architectural Alabaster** light surfaces, **Safety Hazard Amber/Gold** interactive highlights, and **Effluent & Compliance Emerald/Teal** indicators representing verified water recycling and passed audits.
- **Field-Verified Visual Storytelling**: Custom inline vector schematics (chevron-diamond emblem, ETP treatment flow, fire pump/hydrant loop, NFPA 921 cause-mapping matrix) and engineering telemetry readouts (`tabular-nums`) instead of fluffy stock tropes.

### 2.2 Color System (`oklch()` Tokens, `light-dark()`, Contrast & Fallbacks)

All colors must obey [MODERN_WEB_GUIDANCE_GUIDE.md §3](file:///f:/RMC/MODERN_WEB_GUIDANCE_GUIDE.md#L55-L123):
1. Include `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` in every HTML `<head>`.
2. Declare `:root { color-scheme: light dark; }` in `@layer theme`.
3. Provide `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` fallbacks alongside `@supports (color: light-dark(white, black))` progressive enhancement (never register `light-dark()` tokens with `@property`).
4. On dark-overridden bands (`.band--navy`), explicitly set `color-scheme: dark; background-color: ...; color: ...;`.
5. Use `color-mix(in oklab, ...)` for dynamic tints/shades—**never** adjust `L` alone via relative `oklch()`.
6. Pair every status color with an icon or text label (WCAG 1.4.1) and keep `1px solid` borders for `@media (forced-colors: active)`.

### 2.3 Typography & Fluid Type Scale

- **Primary Sans (`--font-sans`)**: `'Plus Jakarta Sans', system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif` — clean geometric engineering clarity for UI, headings, navigation, and dense specifications.
- **Editorial Serif (`--font-serif`)**: `'Instrument Serif', Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif` — reserved strictly for authoritative editorial accents (italicized manifesto lines like *For All EHS Solutions*, *Find the Cause. Prevent the Next.*, and pull-quotes). Never use for small UI labels or tables.
- **Technical Mono (`--font-mono`)**: `'JetBrains Mono', ui-monospace, 'Cascadia Code', monospace` — used with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for NFPA/ISO/SEQS standard codes, KPI metrics, dossier IDs (`RMC-PRJ-01`), phone numbers, and diagnostic/estimator scores.

#### Scale & Readability Rules (`MODERN_WEB_GUIDANCE_GUIDE.md §4`)
- Apply `font-size-adjust: from-font` on web font stacks to prevent CLS on swap.
- All fluid `clamp()` tokens maintain **`max <= 2.5 * min`** (WCAG 1.4.4 200% zoom safety), zero raw `vw` sizing:
  - `--text-display`: `clamp(2.25rem, 1.55rem + 2.6vw, 3.85rem)` (`line-height: 1.08`, `letter-spacing: -0.025em`, `text-wrap: balance`)
  - `--text-h1`: `clamp(1.95rem, 1.4rem + 2vw, 3.1rem)` (`line-height: 1.12`, `text-wrap: balance`)
  - `--text-h2`: `clamp(1.5rem, 1.2rem + 1.2vw, 2.25rem)` (`line-height: 1.18`, `text-wrap: balance`)
  - `--text-h3`: `clamp(1.18rem, 1.05rem + 0.5vw, 1.45rem)` (`line-height: 1.28`, `text-wrap: balance`)
  - `--text-body-lg`: `clamp(1.05rem, 1rem + 0.25vw, 1.2rem)` (`line-height: 1.6`, `text-wrap: pretty`)
  - `--text-body`: `1rem` (`line-height: 1.6`, `text-wrap: pretty`, prose capped at `max-inline-size: 60ch`)
  - `--text-mono-sm`: `0.875rem` (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`)
